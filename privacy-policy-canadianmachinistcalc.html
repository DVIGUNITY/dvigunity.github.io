Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026

CanadianMachinistCalc is a calculator and reference app for machinists. This app is designed to work locally on the user's device.

Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information such as:

  • name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • precise location
  • contacts
  • photos
  • microphone or camera data
  • payment information

The app does not require account creation.

How the App Works

Calculation inputs and reference lookups are processed locally on the device. We do not use the app to intentionally send personal calculation data to our servers.

Third-Party Services

This version of the app does not use advertising services, analytics tools, or third-party tracking technologies for user profiling.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Retention

Because we do not intentionally collect personal information through the app, we do not retain personal user data on our servers.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
